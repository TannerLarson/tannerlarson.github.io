How it works

      
    
        
  1. 
          Validate the commander(s) on Scryfall (must match is:commander,
          not merely be commander-legal as a card).
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          Pull EDHREC’s recommendations and merge them into one pool: creatures, instants,
          sorceries, enchantments, artifacts, planeswalkers, battles, lands / utility lands, plus
          top, high-synergy, and new-card lists.
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          Budget: your Lands input is the land budget. Non-land slots =
          100 − commanders − lands. Synergy % is the share of those non-land slots filled in the
          synergy pass; the rest are filled in the inclusion pass.
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          Synergy pass: sort the pool by EDHREC Synergy and walk top → bottom.
          Add each eligible card (filters below). Lands stop once the land budget is full;
          non-lands stop at the synergy share of the non-land budget.
        
    5. 
        
  5. 
          Inclusion pass: re-sort by inclusion rate and walk again, adding new
          eligible cards until the non-land budget is full (same land cap).
        
    6. 
        
  6. 
          Scryfall refine: confirm land types from Scryfall type lines. If that
          pushes lands over budget, drop the lowest-synergy extras. Top up non-lands again if
          reclassification opened slots.
        
    7. 
        
  7. 
          Basics: whatever slots remain to hit 100 become basic lands, split in
          proportion to colored mana symbols in the commanders + non-lands (Wastes if colorless).
          EDHREC lands from earlier passes already count toward the land budget.
        
    8. 
        
  8. 
          Filters (applied during both passes): skip tutors unless allowed;
          cap Scryfall game changers at your max; skip cards above your max Scryfall USD
          (cheapest positive of nonfoil / foil / etched). Clear the price field for no cap.
          Output is a Moxfield-importable decklist.

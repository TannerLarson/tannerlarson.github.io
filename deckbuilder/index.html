How it works

      
    
        
  1. 
          Validate the commander(s) on Scryfall (must be a legal commander, not merely commander-legal).
        
    2. 
        
  2. 
          Pull EDHREC’s recommendations for that commander and merge them into one ungrouped pool
          (creatures, spells, artifacts, and so on).
        
    3. 
        
  3. 
          Synergy pass: sort the pool by synergy and walk top → bottom, adding
          every card (lands included). Stop when non-lands reach your synergy % of the
          non-land budget (budget = 100 − commanders − lands).
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          Inclusion pass: re-sort by inclusion rate and walk again, adding every
          new card (lands included) until the non-land budget is full.
        
    5. 
        
  5. 
          Basics: whatever slots remain to hit 100 become basic lands,
          biased about +10 percentage points toward the colors you actually spend in the deck.
          (EDHREC lands from the earlier passes already count toward the total.)
        
    6. 
        
  6. 
          Filters: game changers are capped at your max; tutors are skipped unless you allow them.
          Output is a Moxfield-importable decklist.txt.
